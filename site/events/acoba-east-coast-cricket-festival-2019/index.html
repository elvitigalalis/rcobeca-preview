Cricket Festival

ACOBA East Coast Cricket Festival 2019

Saturday, June 15, 2019 · Tall Timbers Park, Monmouth Junction, NJ

Event details

Date
Saturday, June 15, 2019
From dawn to dusk
Venue
Tall Timbers Park
Monmouth Junction, NJ

The Ananda College Old Boys’ Association East Coast presented the 14th Annual East Coast Cricket Festival: cricket, volleyball, kids’ games, food, music and more, from dawn to dusk.

Teams: Ananda College, Royal College, Trinity College, St. John’s College, Maris Stella College, Nalanda College, St. Peter’s College, S. Thomas’ College, Mahanama College and St. Joseph’s College.
