Royal College Old Boys’ East Coast Association

Bringing Old Royalists together to help the students of Royal College

Since 1993, Old Boys of Royal College, Colombo, in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut have met as friends and funded classrooms, labs and scholarships at the College, and relief across Sri Lanka.
